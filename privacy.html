Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What We Collect

Miru is a WhatsApp AI assistant that helps UK families with:

  • School comms: We read school emails (via Gmail OAuth) to extract event details, trips, and reminders
  • Train info: We fetch live train departures using your postcode
  • Fuel prices: We show nearby fuel stations and prices
  • Local info: We fetch your local MP, councillor, and council information

2. Gmail & Email Data

We only read, never store or share email content.

  • Gmail OAuth scope: gmail.readonly (read-only access)
  • We extract: Event dates, times, trip names, deadlines, cancellations
  • We do NOT: Download attachments, share emails with third parties, use emails for training or analytics
  • Email data is processed in-memory and discarded after extraction
  • Extracted events (dates, titles only) are stored in our database for your personal timeline

3. Personal Data We Keep

  • WhatsApp phone number (for authentication)
  • Postcode (for trains, fuel, local info)
  • School names (linked to your account)
  • Extracted school events (dates, titles, trip details)
  • Your preferences (which modules you've enabled)

4. What We Don't Do

  • ❌ Store full email content
  • ❌ Share data with advertisers or brokers
  • ❌ Use your data to train AI models
  • ❌ Sell or rent your information
  • ❌ Track you across the web

5. Third-Party Services

We use these APIs with your permission:

  • Gmail API: Read school emails (OAuth, read-only)
  • Real-Time Trains API: Fetch live train departures
  • Postcodes.io: Convert postcodes to coordinates
  • Parliament UK API: Fetch MP information
  • Supabase: Database storage (PostgreSQL, encrypted in transit)

6. Data Retention

  • School events: Kept until you delete them
  • Postcode/preferences: Kept until account deletion
  • API logs: Retained for 30 days for debugging

7. Your Rights

  • 📥 Export your data (events, preferences)
  • 🗑️ Delete your account and all associated data
  • 🔐 Revoke Gmail access anytime via Google Account settings
  • ❓ Contact us: mekala@gmail.com

8. Security

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Database encryption at rest (Supabase)
  • OAuth 2.0 for secure Gmail access (no password storage)
  • Regular security audits

9. Children's Privacy

Miru is designed for parents/guardians to manage school comms for their own children. We do not collect data directly from children. Parents are responsible for any school information shared via Miru.

10. Changes to This Policy

We'll notify you of major changes via WhatsApp or email. Continued use of Miru after changes means you accept them.
