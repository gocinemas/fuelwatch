## Key Insights Across Markets

### Pricing PPP-Adjusted Pattern

| Brand | UK (GBP) | USA (USD) | India (INR) | PPP Index | Real Tier |
|-------|----------|----------|------------|-----------|-----------|
| Olay | £12.99 | $16.50 | ₹999 | GB:1.0, US:1.0, IN:0.25 | UK/US: mass-prestige \| IN: premium |
| Red Bull | £2.00 | $2.50 | ₹75 | GB:1.0, US:1.0, IN:0.25 | Same premium tier, but different affordability |
| Coca-Cola | £1.50 | $2.50 | ₹40 | GB:1.0, US:1.0, IN:0.25 | Mass-market everywhere, lowest price |

### Positioning & Segment Size

- **Olay:** Mass-prestige in UK/US (target: middle-affluent women) → Premium in India (target: affluent women only)
- **Red Bull:** Premium in all markets, but segment size varies (12M UK, 85M US, 35M India)
- **Coca-Cola:** Mass-market everywhere, largest addressable segment

### Category Growth Signals

- **Skincare:** Mature UK/US (3-3.5% CAGR), emerging India (9.2% CAGR)
- **Energy drinks:** Mature UK/US (4-5.5% CAGR), high-growth India (8.5% CAGR)
- **Soft drinks:** Flat/negative UK/US (-1.2% CAGR), emerging India (5.2% CAGR)

**→ Market entry pattern: Declining mature markets + growing emerging markets = global net growth**
