### 1. **Coca Cola**
**Parent:** The Coca-Cola Company  
**Category:** Beverages  
**Founded:** 1886 | **HQ:** Atlanta, USA

#### Financials (Revenue, Market Cap, Profit)
- **SEC Edgar:** https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000021344&type=10-K
  - Look for: 10-K annual report, Item 1.1 (Net Revenue), Item 7 (Financial Statements)
  - Latest filing date: Usually Feb-Mar of next year
- **Yahoo Finance:** https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/KO
  - Market Cap, P/E Ratio, Dividend Yield, 52-week price
- **Investor Relations:** https://www.coca-colacompany.com/investors
  - Earnings reports, earnings call transcripts

#### Products & Pricing
- **USA:** https://www.amazon.com/s?k=coca+cola (retail pricing)
- **UK:** https://www.tesco.com/groceries/en-GB/search?query=coca+cola (Tesco pricing)
- **India:** https://www.amazon.in/s?k=coca+cola (Amazon India pricing)
- **Official:** https://www.coca-cola.com/en/brands (brand portfolio, products)

#### Competitors & Market Share
- **Statista:** Search "global soft drinks market share" (paid, but snippets free)
- **Market Research:** Mordor Intelligence, Allied Market Research for beverages
- Direct competitors: PepsiCo (Pepsi), Red Bull, Monster Energy

#### Social Media
- Instagram: @cocacola → official follower count
- YouTube: Coca-Cola channel
- TikTok: @cocacola (if applicable)

#### Confidence Scores
- SEC 10-K data: 95%
- Retail pricing: 75% (changes daily)
- Competitor market share: 80% (research firm dependent)
