## Key Insights for Market Entry

### UK Market (Mature, Saturated)
- Skincare: Intense competition at economy/mass-market levels; premium brands capture margins
- Beverages: Coca-Cola/PepsiCo dominate; local heritage (Limca, Thums Up) niche play for diaspora
- Growth: Premiumization over volume; wellness trends (sparkling water, clean skincare)
- Strategy: Differentiate on ingredients (clinically-proven, natural) or lifestyle positioning

### US Market (Mature, Segmented)
- Skincare: Strong mass-prestige category (CeraVe, Olay Regenerist); direct-to-consumer emerging (The Ordinary)
- Beverages: Cola wars still active; energy drinks growing (Monster, Red Bull); juice declining
- Growth: Premium pricing, subscription models, TikTok influencer partnerships
- Strategy: Target underserved segments (sensitive skin, gen-Z ingredient-conscious)

### India Market (Emerging, Rapidly Growing)
- Skincare: International brand prestige (9.2% CAGR); local players emerging in mass-market
- Beverages: Local pride brands strong (Thums Up, Limca); Western import premium positioning
- Growth: Rising disposable income, urban expansion, online penetration increasing
- Strategy: Dual approach - local mass-market volume + premium import positioning for aspirational
