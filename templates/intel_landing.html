What is Intel?

Intel is a real-time brand & company intelligence platform that answers the questions every strategist asks: "Should we enter this market? How viable is our brand here? Which companies are winning?"

Instead of guesswork or generic reports, Intel gives you verified data about real brands and companies across major markets. We analyze positioning, pricing power, distribution reach, market potential, financial health, and competitive dynamics — then tell you exactly what it means for your strategy.

🟢 Company Intelligence (LIVE) — One search shows everything: Financials (EDGAR, Yahoo), Leadership (movements, board changes), Hiring (live job openings).
🟢 Brand Intelligence (LIVE) — Market viability, competitive positioning, growth opportunity for any consumer brand.
No AI hallucinations. No made-up numbers. Just real data from verified sources (SEC, OpenCorporates, LinkedIn, job boards, market data).
