UK Market (Saturated, Premium-focused):
  • Skincare: £4.50-£95.00 range shows clear tier segmentation

  • Growth: Premiumization over volume; wellness trends (sparkling water)
  • Opportunity: Clean ingredients, clinical efficacy claims
  • Segment sizes: 8-10M per tier (total ~76M skincare, ~162M beverages)

US Market (Competitive, DTC-emerging):
  • Skincare: The Ordinary disrupting with clinical + direct-to-consumer
  • Beverages: Energy drinks (Monster) overtaking cola; juice declining
  • Growth: Premium pricing, influencer marketing (TikTok), subscriptions
  • Opportunity: Ingredient transparency, sustainability, gen-Z targeting
  • Segment sizes: 40-50M per tier (total ~336M skincare, ~560M beverages)

India Market (High-growth, Aspiration-driven):
  • Skincare: 9.2% CAGR; international brands premium positioned
  • Beverages: Local pride strong (Thums Up, Limca); Western imports premium
  • Growth: Rising disposable income; urban expansion; online penetration
  • Opportunity: Dual strategy - local mass-market + premium import positioning
  • Segment sizes: 20-40M for beverages, 3-22M for skincare
